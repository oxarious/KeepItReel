# Explained post flags — 0.3.12

Version 0.3.19 replaces the separate YouTube creator and hide controls with one coloured monkey on supported cards, watch players and active Shorts. It opens local analysis, observed reasons, Hide video and Block creator (when identity is available). Existing Undo and filtering preferences remain. Green means no matching patterns in checked text, yellow means a disclosure, possible concern or limited check, and red means a concrete secret or prize-payment request. No colour certifies safety, creator reputation or AI authorship. Dismissed findings remain available in analysis.

On YouTube, open the monkey for the flag reasons and dismiss action. The older inline flag descriptions below apply to TikTok. Filter covers still explain why an item was hidden; the video analysis remains available from their monkey. Video frames/audio are not analysed. Only supported published text and platform disclosures are inspected.

Supported desktop YouTube cards, watch pages, active Shorts and TikTok posts can show expandable notices. These checks do not analyse pixels/voices or determine whether undisclosed content was generated by AI. No cloud service, malware scan or new site permission is added.

## Notices

| Notice | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Flagged · AI disclosure | A recognised platform label, or a disclosure observed on this device within the existing 30-day cache window. Reasons identify remembered evidence. |
| Flagged · Possible spam | Specific English published-text patterns, or repeated promotional text observed in this tab. Each matched reason is shown. This does not establish AI authorship or malicious intent. |

Spam patterns cover a direct request for a password/code/recovery phrase, a prize claim combined with a payment request, and guaranteed/risk-free earnings combined with a contact/promotional-link request. Obvious safety-education phrases suppress these checks. Ordinary AI keywords, polished writing, generic promotion and a single repeated creator do not establish spam.

Repetition requires the same normalized promotional text (at least 60 characters, including contact and financial/promotion terms) across at least three distinct post IDs and at least two known creators. Case, Unicode compatibility forms and whitespace are normalized. It is exact normalized-text matching, not a semantic similarity model or an account-wide investigation. Reposts and legitimate promotions can match.

## Choices

- Check posts for spam patterns defaults on. Disabling it clears the comparison memory and removes spam findings; creator rules and AI-disclosure preferences remain independent.
- Hide suspicious posts automatically defaults off, including migration of existing installs. On applies the selected Cover/Collapse/Label display mode to possible-spam matches. Label only keeps content visible and does not auto-skip.
- Filter platform AI disclosures keeps its existing saved/default preference. Turn it off to show a disclosure notice without an automatic cover.
- Always allow keeps a creator visible despite automatic disclosure/spam hiding; an explained notice can remain. Explicit creator blocks take precedence.
- Regular YouTube thumbnails also offer Hide this video without requiring a flag or known creator. It uses the same temporary post-hide/Undo behavior. A creator link appearing later does not undo an explicit hide. The monkey remains a separate creator-block action; names without profile links are not guessed.
- Hide this post covers that item in the current page and offers persistent Undo. It does not add a creator rule. This explicit per-post choice uses a cover even in Label only mode. Show once also restores visibility; neither action restarts playback. A newer post choice invalidates its earlier Undo.
- Dismiss flag suppresses the matching findings for that post in this page. Different findings can produce a new notice. Refresh/closure clears temporary dismiss/hide choices.
- The monkey still blocks the resolved creator, including when a post is already covered. Creator Undo restores the prior rule and rejects a stale action after a newer edit.
- Flags on visible posts do not pause or skip media. Hiding can pause media; the separately enabled auto-skip setting may advance supported short-video feeds. Undo survives that navigation without jumping back or starting playback.

## Scope and data

The scanner samples up to 200 supported posts per pass and reads up to 4,000 characters from recognised published title/caption components. Unrelated comments, recognised editors/forms and KeepItReel controls are excluded. An unknown layout can be missed. Metadata is checked again before a hide/dismiss/block click acts on a recycled player.

The comparison buffer holds at most 200 normalized samples in page memory. Samples expire from matching after 15 minutes and are pruned on later scans. Disabling spam checks, reload or closure clears it. Temporary hide and dismissal maps each hold at most 500 entries. No additional caption archive or numeric AI/spam score is stored or uploaded. Existing optional history can retain an item title (up to 250 characters), URL, creator and filter reason; a TikTok title can come from its caption. See PRIVACY.md.

These local patterns have controlled regression coverage, not measured real-world precision/recall. Obfuscation, language, context and platform changes limit coverage. The normal Chrome owner acceptance steps in BETA-TESTING.md remain required before inviting testers.
