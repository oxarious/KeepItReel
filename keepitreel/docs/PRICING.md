# Free beta and possible paid tier

0.3.20 is free with no login, billing, card or automatic conversion. Do not build subscriptions during this pilot.

| Tier | Proposed contents | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KeepItReel Free | Local disclosure/source filtering, basic link checks, local checker, explanations and controls | Included free in beta |
| KeepItReel Plus — proposed $5 USD/month | Potential opt-in commercial reputation checks, deeper media provenance, cross-device rules and advanced controls | Research proposal only; not implemented or sold |

Keep useful protection free. Charge for recurring value/cost after users demonstrate demand. Mobile needs its own supported browser/share-sheet scope; an extension cannot protect every unrelated native app.

After a stable pilot, interview users about the particular feature they would pay for and whether $5/month fits. A non-binding interest list must be labelled as such. Build one advanced feature with explicit consent, measured cost and honest coverage before billing.

Planning example only: target service cost at or below $1 of $5 gross monthly revenue per active paid user, leaving $4 before fees, taxes, refunds, support, acquisition and engineering. This is an internal target, not a forecast. Calculate worst-case usage and licensing before quotas. Never promise unlimited paid API scans. Resolve seller/customer tax and payment requirements before charging.

## Beta costs

No inference API, application server, database, authentication or billing service is needed. Architecture service runtime cost: $0 per local scan. Other costs: Chrome's one-time registration fee shown at signup, optional domain/static privacy hosting, support tooling and testing/development time. No service or subscription was purchased.

Cloud checks change costs and privacy. Google's Safe Browsing API is for non-commercial use; Web Risk is a commercial option to evaluate. Neither is implemented.
References: https://developers.google.com/safe-browsing ; https://docs.cloud.google.com/web-risk/docs/overview
