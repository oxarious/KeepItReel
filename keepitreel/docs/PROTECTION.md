# Protection scope — 0.3.20

Version 0.3.20 adds per-tab toolbar warning badges and a Show monkeys on websites switch. Turning the switch off hides the page, inbox, opened-email and feed launchers; enabled scans, automatic filters, Undo notices and click-time link warnings remain active. The toolbar number counts distinct warning types, not videos, emails or blocked ads. Its popup explains the categories. Red is reserved for secret/prize-payment request categories; other matched categories use amber. No badge is not a safety verdict.

Version 0.3.19 replaces the separate YouTube creator and hide controls with one coloured monkey on supported cards, watch players and active Shorts. It opens local analysis, observed reasons, Hide video and Block creator (when identity is available). Existing Undo and filtering preferences remain. Green means no matching patterns in checked text, yellow means a disclosure, possible concern or limited check, and red means a concrete secret or prize-payment request. No colour certifies safety, creator reputation or AI authorship. Dismissed findings remain available in analysis.

Version 0.3.18 adds an Outlook inbox corner fallback when a recognised message row has no semantic timestamp. A small 24px target sits at the lower trailing corner, below the time in the tested layout, with reserved horizontal space. Existing timestamp placements remain supported. Row styles are restored when checks stop, and outlook.cloud.microsoft now shares the existing mail-host allowlist. No permissions or message-data storage are added.

Version 0.3.17 fixes email tooltip clipping. Hover/focus text uses the browser top layer, is positioned within the viewport and remains readable outside clipped inbox rows. It can be hovered and dismissed with Escape, hides when analysis opens or the page scrolls, and is removed with its control. Only one email hint is shown at a time. Without Popover support, the accessible button label and click report remain available.

Version 0.3.16 fixes Gmail hover actions covering the inbox monkey. Gmail indicators now occupy a separate 34px trailing cell after the received-time area. A row-scoped rule reserves that space beside Gmail’s hover toolbar; the time can hide without hiding the monkey. Row height and native Archive/Delete/Unread/Snooze actions are preserved. The added cell, row marker and stylesheet are removed when checks stop. Outlook keeps its existing compact time placement.

Version 0.3.15 places the inbox monkey after the received time. Its visible mark is smaller (15 by 20 CSS pixels), with a 24 by 24 keyboard-accessible target. The target is positioned inside a zero-height inline slot so it cannot enlarge the time line. Timestamp wrapping is temporarily prevented and the original style is restored on removal. Unknown timestamp layouts are left untouched. Opened-email buttons and analysis are unchanged.

Version 0.3.14 adds local inbox-preview monkey buttons to supported Gmail and Outlook rows. Red indicates a high-risk secret request; yellow indicates another suspicious request; grey means preview-only information, never safe. Click the monkey for reasons without opening or selecting the email. Only rendered row text is assessed, capped at 100 supported rows; hidden bodies, actual link destinations, attachments and sender authentication are not checked in the inbox. English and Swedish request patterns are supported. Opened-email analysis and its colours remain separate. No new permission, mailbox API, network service or stored message data is added.

Version 0.3.13 replaces the wide bottom-of-email notice with a 44px monkey button before each supported opened message body. Click to open a compact report with explanations, Dismiss/Show warning and incorrect-warning reporting. Green means no matching warning patterns in a complete local check; yellow means concerns, link notes or partial coverage; red means a private-code/password/recovery-phrase request. These colours are pattern indicators, not safety or malware verdicts. Dismissal does not change the colour. Escape, outside click and Close dismiss the report. No permissions, network service or persistent data types changed.

Supported YouTube/TikTok feed posts now have explained AI-disclosure and possible-spam flags. Spam checks inspect up to 200 published title/caption samples per pass, using specific English secret-request, prize-fee and guaranteed-return patterns. Repetition requires matching normalized promotional text across at least three distinct post IDs and multiple known creators observed in the current tab within 15 minutes. Posts stay visible by default; automatic suspicious-post hiding is optional. These patterns can miss spam and flag legitimate content. They do not establish AI origin, automation, malicious intent or malware. No video, voice, attachment or reputation analysis is added. See FLAGGED-POSTS.md.

Website scanning is optional. It looks for explicit English AI self-disclosures in readable text and checks visible links locally. No disclosure means origin unknown. It does not infer authorship from style, analyse video/audio/image pixels, verify provenance signatures or fetch malware reputation.

Signals include displayed-address disagreement, URL credentials, misleading brand subdomains, internationalised/numeric domains, short links and HTTP. Some are informational notices. Higher-concern ordinary clicks show a review dialog. Redirects, scripts, downloads, every navigation path and attachments are not comprehensively covered.

Gmail/Outlook checks are experimental, off by default and independently enabled. Supported opened bodies are checked for English and Swedish requests for secrets, unusual payments, upfront fees or guaranteed returns (English); available Gmail sender labels can be compared with addresses. No finding proves safety or human authorship. Drafts, recognised quotes and form values are excluded. Recognised Outlook reply-divider content, including following quoted siblings, is excluded from both text checks and link interception; replies written below that divider are therefore not assessed. Unsupported layouts show a coverage limit. Each supported opened body has its own status: Flagged email for review-level findings, Link notes for informational link properties, Opened message checked when no patterns match, or Partial email check for unreadable/over-limit text. None is a safety verdict. Reasons stay scoped to that message.

Dismiss for this message leaves a compact Show warning control. It is temporary, tab-local, and does not whitelist the sender or bypass link checks. Changing the message content, inspected links, available sender metadata, provider message identifier or page URL invalidates dismissal. Reloading, closing the tab or disabling checks resets it; a newly mounted body may be assessed anew. Metadata availability differs by layout, so identity is not authenticated.

Report incorrect warning opens Beta & feedback with only that message's warning categories. Review the preview, optionally export it, and share it yourself through your agreed contact channel. Nothing is submitted automatically. Separate pending reports have isolated draft keys.

LinkedIn is excluded at registration and execution; no invitation adapter ships. KeepItReel does not authenticate profiles, accept requests, delete messages or control native phone apps.

Checks are bounded, show partial coverage, debounce changes and defer hidden-tab scans. Local pasted text and each opened email are capped at 30,000 characters (with a 6,000-node DOM traversal limit); page links at 750 and supported opened mail roots at 40. This is not a real-time guarantee on every mutation.

General checks pause on recognised sensitive forms and selected workspace/account hosts. Users can exclude additional private sites. This is not a comprehensive sensitive-content classifier. Findings are transient; feed disclosures and history have separate retention controls in PRIVACY.md.


## Corner button controls

Pause on this site adds the exact host to your excluded sites, including its subdomains. The action becomes Start on this site and removes that exact exclusion. It does not remove a broader inherited exclusion, bypass a sensitive-page pause or grant browser permissions. Use Protection settings for those separate controls.

Hide this button hides the corner logo across supported pages and remains saved after refresh. Checks and link-review dialogs continue. Restore it using Show page button in the Chrome toolbar popup. You can also open the page report from the popup while its corner logo is hidden.

Inbox preview checks share the separately enabled email switch. They assess at most 100 supported rendered rows with at most 2,400 characters per row. Preview snapshots exist only in tab memory; the extension neither fetches unopened bodies nor marks rows read. Unsupported rows have no monkey; absence of a monkey is not a passed check. Red is reserved for explicit secret requests; other matching requests are yellow and no match is grey. Actual message link checks require opening the email.
